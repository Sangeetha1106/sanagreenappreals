How an order moves

Four steps, no surprises

Whether it is 200 pieces or a few thousand, every order follows the same path — and you know where it stands.

1

Tell us what you need

Share styles, sizes, colours and quantity by WhatsApp or phone. Photos and reference garments help.

2

Sample & approve

We prepare a sample against your brief. You check fit and fabric, and we correct before cutting.

3

Cut & stitch

Fabric is cut to the approved pattern and stitched, with checks at each stage of the line.

4

Finish & dispatch

Trimming, pressing, folding and packing — then dispatch with the delivery date we agreed.
