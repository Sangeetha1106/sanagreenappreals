About Sana Green Apparels

Rooted in Salem’s textile heart with an eye on sustainable quality.

Sana Green Apparels is a registered partnership apparel manufacturing firm based on Cuddalore Main Road, Ammapet, Salem — led by managing partner Nithyanatham Subramani and partner Perumal Anbalagan.

Salem has long been renowned for its textiles and fabric excellence. We carry forward this rich legacy by uniting modern eco-friendly woven fabric processing with meticulous cut-and-sew apparel production. From single-jersey knitwear to structured woven corporate shirts and soft organic towels, we provide dependable bulk garment solutions with clear delivery dates and verified GST billing.

End-to-End Production

From Fabric to Finished Garments

Sana Green Apparels transforms textile materials into finished, market-ready garments for shops, institutions, and growing apparel labels — combining specialized fabric processing with disciplined manufacturing lines.

Our Defined Expertise: We specialize in eco-conscious fabric processing and complete cut-and-sew garment manufacturing. We do not spin yarn or weave raw fabric — ensuring our full focus remains on meticulous fabric preparation, precision cutting, flawless stitching, and reliable delivery.

Eco Mill Capabilities

10-Step Woven Fabric Processing

Our complete sustainable woven fabric processing line ensures every meter of textile reaches superior hand-feel, colour retention, and minimal residual shrinkage.

01

Grey Inspection

Every meter of raw woven fabric is thoroughly inspected for flaws, defects, and weaving faults. This ensures only the best quality fabric enters our sustainable processing line.

02

Singeing

Surface fibres and fuzz are burnt off using controlled singeing to create a smooth, clean, and even fabric surface.

03

Desizing

Sizing agents applied during weaving are removed using biodegradable enzymes. This prepares the fabric for better absorbency and chemical treatment.

04

Scouring

We clean impurities, natural oils, and wax through eco-friendly scouring. This improves fabric absorbency and softness.

05

Finishing (Stenter)

Using advanced stenter machines, we heat-set the fabric to stabilize width, control GSM, and ensure consistent texture and dimensional stability.

06

Special Finishes

Customized eco-finishes to match exact client requirements:

Softening Water-repellent Anti-bacterial Anti-static Wrinkle-free

All done with GOTS-approved non-toxic chemicals.

07

Fabric Raising & Brushing Machine

Our raising and brushing technology lifts fibres to create a soft, fluffy, and warm surface. Ideal for premium apparel, shackets, and winter wear collections.

08

Sanforizer / Compressive Shrinking Range

To control residual shrinkage, we use compressive shrinking technology. This guarantees less than 3% shrinkage and long-lasting dimensional stability after washing.

09

Final QC – Quality Control

Every roll undergoes 100% quality inspection for shade, shrinkage, strength, pilling, and hand-feel before packing.

10

Despatch

Eco-friendly packing and ready-to-cut dispatch for apparel manufacturing. Reduced plastic and recyclable packaging.

Sustainable Processing Standard: All ten steps are operated under strict environmental compliance with low chemical footprint and energy-efficient water recycling.
Our Commitment

Why Sana Green Apparels?

Six grounded principles guiding how we treat textiles, respect the environment, and serve our bulk partners.

Sustainable and Eco-Friendly Processing

We align our fabric processing with sustainable textile standards, reducing harmful effluent while producing soft, durable, skin-friendly fabrics.

Low-Water Process

Using low-liquor ratio processing machines and systematic water recycling, we drastically curtail freshwater consumption per kilogram of textile processed.

Non-Toxic Chemicals

Biodegradable enzymes, GOTS-compliant softening agents, and azo-free dyes protect fabric integrity, employee health, and end-consumer safety.

Premium Quality Guaranteed

Rigid multi-point inspections ensure precise GSM, dimensional stability with less than 3% residual shrinkage, and consistent batch shades.

Responsible Production

Direct communication with the operating partners, honest turnaround dates, fair working conditions, and clean GST invoicing for every dispatch.

Reduce, Reuse and Recycle

Intelligent CAD marker cutting to minimize textile scraps, recyclable carton packaging, and zero avoidable single-use plastics.

Quality Assurance

Inspected on the table. Verified before packing.

We protect your brand by checking fabrics before the blade touches them, and checking finished garments before the carton is taped.

  • Grey & Finished Fabric Audit

    Rolls are checked on illuminated inspection tables for weave faults, shade banding, and hand-feel.

  • Precision Measurement Check

    Tolerance audits against approved spec sheets ensure size consistency from the 1st to the 1,000th piece.

  • Stitch Integrity & Seam Strength

    Critical load seams, collar alignments, and plackets are tested for stress and clean tension.

  • Regular GST Invoicing

    Fully compliant registered partnership firm with transparent invoicing for seamless enterprise accounting.
