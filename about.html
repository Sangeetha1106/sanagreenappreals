About us

A Salem apparel firm built on steady hands.

Sana Green Apparels is a partnership firm based on Cuddalore Main Road, Ammapet — making garments with attention to fit, finish and delivery.

Where we come from

Managing Director Sana Green Apparels · Salem
Leadership

We started with a simple idea: clothing should be made well, billed properly and delivered when promised.

Sana Green Apparels was registered in April 2025 as a partnership, and operates from a single principal place of business in Ammapet, Salem. The business is led by its managing partner, Nithyanatham Subramani, together with partner Perumal Anbalagan — both residents of Tamil Nadu.

Salem has long been a textile town. We work within that tradition: choosing fabric carefully, keeping patterns consistent from sample to bulk, and treating each order — large or small — as a repeat customer in the making.

Our operations combine sustainable 10-step woven fabric processing (including grey inspection, singeing, enzymatic desizing, eco-scouring, stenter heat-setting, and sanforization for less than 3% shrinkage) with dedicated cut-and-sew apparel lines for T-shirts, polos, hoodies, track pants, uniforms, towels, and kidswear. We strictly focus on fabric processing and finished garment manufacturing, maintaining clarity and quality without unsubstantiated claims.

The leaf in our logo is deliberate. We want to make clothing that is comfortable on people and considerate of the places it comes from.
