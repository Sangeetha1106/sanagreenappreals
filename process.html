Our process

From first message to final carton.

A clear path for every order, so you always know what happens next — and when.

1

Brief

We start by understanding what you need and who it is for.

  • Styles, sizes and colours
  • Approximate quantity and target date
  • Reference photo or sample, if you have one
2

Fabric & sampling

Fabric is chosen against your brief, and a sample is prepared for approval.

  • Shade and feel reviewed on the roll
  • Sample made to your size spec
  • Changes agreed before bulk cutting
3

Cutting

Approved patterns are laid out and cut with care to reduce waste.

  • Pattern and size ratio confirmed
  • Efficient marker layout
  • Cut pieces bundled and labelled
4

Stitching

Garments are assembled on the line with checks along the way.

  • Seams and stitch density inspected
  • Collars, plackets and pockets aligned
  • Defects fixed before moving ahead
5

Finishing & dispatch

Final trimming, pressing and packing, then out the door on the agreed date.

  • Thread trimming and final inspection
  • Folding and packing to your requirement
  • GST invoice and dispatch update
Quality checks

Where we look closely

Four checkpoints that keep an order consistent from the first piece to the last.

Fabric inspection

Rolls are examined for shade variation, flaws and feel before they reach the cutting table.

Measurement audit

Finished pieces are measured against the approved sample and size chart.

Stitch & seam check

Seams, hems and button attachments are checked for strength and neatness.

Final inspection

Every batch is reviewed for cleanliness, trims and packing before it leaves.

Good to know

Common questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

It depends on the style, fabric and customisation. Message us your requirement and we’ll tell you what works — we are happy to discuss smaller trial orders too.

Can you match a garment I already have?

Yes. Share a sample or clear photos with measurements and we’ll prepare a sample for approval before going into bulk.

Do you offer private labels and custom packing?

Labels, tags and packing can be arranged to suit your brand. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll confirm what’s possible.

How long does an order take?

Timelines depend on quantity, fabric availability and approvals. We give you a date when we quote, and update you if anything shifts.

Will I get a GST invoice?

Yes. We are registered under GST (regular registration), and every order is billed with a proper tax invoice.
